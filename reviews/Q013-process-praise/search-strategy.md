# Q013 Search Strategy

Last searched: 2026-09-26

## Review type

Targeted evidence review. This is **not** a de novo systematic review.

## Search streams

1. child praise / intrinsic motivation reviews
2. preschool process vs person praise experiments
3. naturalistic parent-praise longitudinal studies
4. praise timing / specificity in toddlers
5. descriptive/general praise as reinforcement
6. recent replication / contradictory studies
7. 2023–2026 process-person praise updates
8. Japan-specific young-child praise research

## Fresh update — 2026-09-26

Verified:

Newly retained:
- Xu et al. 2024 preschool praise-balance study

Targeted search did not identify a newer systematic review/meta-analysis that clearly supersedes the Henderlong & Lepper conceptual synthesis specifically for parent process-vs-person praise in ages 0–6. This is a targeted-search result, not proof none exists.

## Extraction rules

Separate:
- process / person / product / general praise
- generic vs nongeneric wording
- effort vs strategy vs action
- praise amount vs praise type
- caregiver vs experimenter
- naturalistic vs role-play/lab
- short-term post-failure response vs later mindset/achievement
- motivation vs behavioral reinforcement

## Interpretation rules

Do not convert:
- one experiment → universal parenting rule
- N=53 observational cohort → causal prescription
- 2013 + 2018 same cohort → two independent replications
- process praise → “say effort praise constantly”
- Japan-context praise study → direct process-vs-person evidence
- older-child null result → proof preschool effects are absent

## Limitations

- heterogeneous praise definitions and outcomes
- classic evidence often uses short experimental scenarios
- long-term parent evidence is heavily dependent on one small cohort
- culture / language transportability is limited
- direct Japan-specific process-vs-person evidence remains limited
