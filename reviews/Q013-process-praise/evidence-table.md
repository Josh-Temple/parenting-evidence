## Core boundaries

- A4 and A5 are the **same N=53 longitudinal cohort**
- experimental short-term coping ≠ long-term academic achievement
- person / process / product / general / generic praise are not interchangeable categories
- praise type ≠ praise amount
- parent praise ≠ experimenter praise
- motivation / mindset ≠ behavioral reinforcement
- Japan-context evidence does not directly answer process-vs-person causality
