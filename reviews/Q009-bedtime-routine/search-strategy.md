# Q009 Search Strategy

Last searched: 2026-09-26

## Review type

Targeted evidence review. This is **not** a de novo systematic review.

Direct intervention anchor:
- Mindell et al. 2009

Synthesis anchor:
- Mindell & Williamson 2018

Update horizon:
- through 2026-09-26

## Question decomposition

Population:
- infants, toddlers and preschool children, approximately 6 months–6 years

Primary exposure/intervention:
- consistent pre-sleep activity sequence before lights out

Keep separate:
- routine consistency/frequency
- routine content/components
- screen/device avoidance
- bedtime timing
- parent presence / sleep associations
- behavioral sleep-training procedures

Primary outcomes:
- sleep onset latency
- night waking
- wake after sleep onset
- sleep continuity
- total/nighttime sleep
- caregiver-rated sleep problems

Secondary:
- caregiver mood/stress
- social-emotional outcomes
- broader development

## Search streams

1. direct bedtime-routine intervention
2. consistency/frequency
3. longitudinal evidence
4. Japan-specific evidence
5. recent randomized evidence
6. component-specific evidence
7. funding/conflict verification
8. 2025–2026 systematic-review update

Use boundary:
This systematic review maps 59 early-childhood behavioral sleep interventions and reports bedtime routines in 53% of programs. It does not isolate bedtime-routine efficacy and is therefore retained as methodological/context evidence, not as a new direct effect estimate.

## Inclusion principles

Include:
- direct routine interventions
- large observational consistency studies
- longitudinal temporal evidence
- Japan-specific evidence
- recent randomized updates
- component studies only when clearly separated
- funding/conflict context where material

Separate/exclude from primary inference:
- Q004 extinction/controlled-crying/bedtime-fading evidence
- broad sleep-hygiene education as if identical to bedtime routine
- individual component effect as proof of package effect
- broader developmental associations as routine-intervention causality

## Search limitations

- no database-native full export
- no dual screening
- no project-wide formal risk-of-bias tool
- evidence network has overlapping investigators/funding
- many outcomes are parent-reported
- age/cultural transportability is limited
