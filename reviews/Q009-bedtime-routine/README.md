# Q009 — Bedtime routine and sleep in young children

Parent-facing question:

**毎晩、決まった就寝前ルーティンを作ると、乳幼児の睡眠は良くなるか。**

Research state: DRAFT  
Formalized: 2026-09-26

## Scope

Primary population:
- infants, toddlers and preschool children, roughly 6 months–6 years

Primary intervention/exposure:
- a consistent sequence of activities before lights out

Keep separate:
- routine consistency/frequency
- bedtime timing
- individual components such as bath, reading, soothing and toothbrushing
- screen/device avoidance
- parent presence / sleep-association components
- Q004 behavioral sleep-training procedures

Primary outcomes:
- sleep onset latency
- night waking number/duration
- sleep continuity
- total sleep
- parent-rated sleep problems

Secondary outcomes remain separate:
- caregiver mood/stress
- child social-emotional outcomes
- broader development
