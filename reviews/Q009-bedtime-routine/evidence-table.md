## Detailed verification targets

### A1 Mindell 2009
- N=405 total
- infant 7–18m: n=206
- toddler 18–36m: n=199
- 3-week randomized studies
- baseline differences existed for several sleep variables despite randomization
- primary reporting emphasized within-group repeated-measures change; do not overstate a clean between-group effect estimate
- package effect only
- parent-report sleep
- short-term outcome
- commercial funding/context retained

### J1 Yamamoto & Hara 2024
- overall BTR → CSHQ sleep disturbance: β=-0.013, p=0.736
- overall BTR → SDQ total difficulties: β=-0.063, p=0.094
- reading/story → CSHQ: β=+0.228, p=0.006
- device avoidance → CSHQ: β=-0.222, p=0.011
- item-level analyses exploratory
- reading coefficient is not interpreted causally as harm

### U1 Mindell 2026
- randomized: 100
- analyzed: 86
- completed both 15m and 24m: 62
- overall sleep main effects: null
- overall social-emotional main effects: null
- overall caregiver-stress main effects: null
- usual-care missingness 52% vs intervention 28%, p=0.014
- interaction analyses used p<0.10 due exploratory nature / limited power
- reading at 15m higher intervention
- bath/toothbrushing differences not significant at 15m
- no component differences at 24m
- subgroup results do not override overall null

## Interpretation rules

- direct 2009 effect stays short-term and package-level
- observational consistency evidence does not create a causal frequency threshold
- component associations are not the whole-routine causal mechanism
- Japan overall-null result remains visible
- 2026 overall-null RCT result remains visible
- subgroup/moderation findings remain exploratory
- funding/conflicts are context, not automatic exclusion
- overlapping research network is not counted as fully independent replication
- bedtime routine is kept separate from Q004 sleep training
