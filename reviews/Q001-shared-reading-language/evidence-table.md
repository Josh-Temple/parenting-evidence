## Interpretation notes

### C001 — association

Supported.

The strongest recent synthesis of home-based exposure reports consistent positive associations between shared reading and language/vocabulary. The 2026 JECS result materially improves directness to Japanese families, while remaining observational.

### C002 — causal intervention effect

Partially supported.

RCT meta-analysis estimates favor reading interventions, but the estimated effect becomes much smaller when studies use active comparison groups. The evidence is compatible with a small average causal benefit, but does not justify a large-effect claim.

### C003 — special technique superiority

Uncertain.

Interactive/dialogic training clearly changes caregiver behaviour. Direct evidence that it improves child language beyond ordinary active shared reading is inconsistent.

### C004 — earlier is better

Insufficient for a causal claim.

Starting literacy promotion from birth rather than six months improved the early home reading environment in one RCT but did not produce a measurable language advantage at 24 months. Observational age-of-onset findings are vulnerable to confounding.

### C005 — long-term persistence

Uncertain.

The 2019 intervention meta-analysis did not find a statistically significant follow-up effect. Longer-term language outcomes remain insufficiently established.
