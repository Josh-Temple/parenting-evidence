## A2 milestone detail

Muszyńska et al. 2025:
- bilingual n=302
- monolingual matched n=302
- entry mean age 12.78 months; range 0–24 months
- no clear group difference in:
  - babbling
  - first word
  - 10th word
  - first multiword utterance
- evidence for later milestones, especially 50th word, is less definitive
- bilingual production was considered across both languages

Interpretation:
Strong evidence against a simple early global-delay claim, but not proof that every language-specific score is identical.

## Measurement boundary

Keep distinct:
- vocabulary in Japanese alone
- vocabulary in English alone
- total vocabulary across both languages
- total conceptual vocabulary across both languages

Do not translate one-language difference into global developmental delay.

## Exposure boundary

Interactive bilingual exposure is not equivalent to passive video/audio.

No inference about English videos/apps should be made from caregiver/childcare interaction evidence.

## Japan boundary

Direct Japanese-English intervention/longitudinal evidence:
**NOT IDENTIFIED IN TARGETED SEARCH**

This is a search finding, not a nonexistence claim.
