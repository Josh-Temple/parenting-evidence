## Review question

同じ野菜へのrepeated taste exposureは、4か月〜6歳の子どもで、その野菜へのacceptance / liking / intakeを増やすか。

## Claim map

- C001: target-food acceptance / intake
- C002a: different vegetable generalization, 4–24m
- C002b: different vegetable generalization, 2–6y
- C002c: overall diet / picky eating / nutrition / growth
- C003: universal number / frequency
- C004: non-taste exposure, 2–6y willingness-to-try
- C005: non-food reinforcement
- C006: treatment of overall picky eating
- C007: clinical boundary

## Core concepts

```text
(infant* OR toddler* OR preschool* OR child*)
AND
(repeated exposure OR repeated taste exposure OR food exposure OR familiarization)
AND
(vegetable* OR food* OR picky eating OR food neophobia)
AND
(acceptance OR liking OR intake OR willingness to try)
```

## Inclusion

- Population: primary 4 months–6 years
- Intervention: repeated taste exposure to a target food; secondary non-taste exposure or non-food reinforcement
- Comparator: no/usual exposure, fewer exposures, exposure alone, alternative schedule or reinforcement condition
- Outcomes: willingness to taste, liking, actual intake, different-food generalization, persistence
- Study designs: systematic review/meta-analysis, RCT, controlled intervention; official guidance for practical context
- Update date window: 2023-06-01 through 2026-09-24
- Language: English/Japanese where accessible

## Exclusion

- school-age/adolescent-only populations outside primary scope
- multicomponent programs where repeated exposure cannot be separated, except background
- PFD / ARFID clinical treatment studies as direct ordinary-picky-eating evidence
- observational picky-eating associations as proof of intervention efficacy
- exposure counts used only to create a universal threshold

## Main anchor evidence

### 2025 Dietary Guidelines Advisory Committee / USDA NESR

- review context: 2025 Dietary Guidelines Advisory Committee
- Suggested Citation: November 2024
- literature coverage:
  - infants/young children: January 1980–May 2023
  - children 2–6 years: January 2000–May 2023
- key conclusion: target vegetable acceptance Grade Moderate at 4–24m and 2–6y
- generalization:
  - 4–24m different vegetable: Grade Moderate
  - 2–6y different vegetable: Grade Limited
- non-taste exposure:
  - 2–6y willingness to try: Grade Moderate
  - 4–24m: Grade Not Assignable
- source: https://nesr.usda.gov/2025-dietary-guidelines-advisory-committee-systematic-reviews/repeated-exposure_acceptance

Important: committee cycle、Suggested Citation、literature search end dateを同一視しない。

### Appleton et al. 2018

- systematic review/meta-analysis
- 43 articles / 117 comparisons
- liking: SMD 0.35, 95% CI 0.17–0.53
- intake: SMD 0.23, 95% CI 0.07–0.39
- average effects small; long-term evidence limited
- PMID 30321277; DOI 10.1093/ajcn/nqy143
- erratum PMID 30517611 / DOI 10.1093/ajcn/nqy350: citation/wording corrections; no reported change to Q007 effect estimates
