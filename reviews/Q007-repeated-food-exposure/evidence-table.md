PMID 30517611 / DOI 10.1093/ajcn/nqy350は、initial publicationで生じたin-text citation errorsと複数のwording errorsを修正したerratum。erratumはQ007で用いるmeta-analytic effect estimatesの訂正を報告していない。Q007はcorrected online articleの数値を使用する。

## Interpretation by claim

### C001 — target-food acceptance / intake

Status: SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARIES

- What supports the claim: A1 external Grade Moderate in both age groups; A2 small positive pooled effects; D2
- What limits the claim: average effect small; food-specific heterogeneity; limited long-term evidence; D3 null for courgette
- Comparator: no/lower exposure or pre-intervention depending on study
- Outcome: target-food acceptance, liking, actual intake
- Causal boundary: controlled intervention evidence supports a target-food effect; does not establish whole-diet or growth benefit
- Japan applicability: direct Japanese repeated-target-food RCT not identified in targeted search

### C002a — different vegetable, age 4–24 months

Status: SUPPORTED — AGE/FOOD-CATEGORY SPECIFIC

- A1: different vegetable acceptance Grade Moderate
- Do not generalize to fruit or overall diet

### C002b — different vegetable, age 2–6 years

Status: LIMITED

- A1: external Grade Limited
- evidence more heterogeneous than target-food acceptance

### C002c — overall picky eating / diet / nutrition / growth

Status: NOT ESTABLISHED

- B1 documents the burden/associations of picky eating, not treatment effect
- no reviewed source establishes repeated exposure alone as a treatment of the overall phenotype

### C003 — universal number / frequency

Status: NOT ESTABLISHED

- D1 found no particular frequency could be recommended
- U1 suggests schedule can matter even with the same total exposure count
- study-specific plateaus or common exposure counts are not biological thresholds

### C004 — non-taste exposure

Status: SUPPORTED FOR WILLINGNESS TO TRY — 2–6Y ONLY

- A1 external Grade Moderate for willingness to try in ages 2–6
- 4–24m: Grade Not Assignable
- willingness to try ≠ liking ≠ actual intake

### C005 — positive non-food reinforcement

Status: PROMISING / STRATEGY-SPECIFIC

- U2 supports possible add-on benefit
- not a universal parenting recommendation
- non-food reinforcement ≠ food bribe

### C007 — clinical boundary

Status: SEPARATE

- C1 defines PFD through functional impairment across medical, nutritional, feeding-skill and psychosocial domains
- C2 clarifies that PFD and ARFID can overlap but are not interchangeable diagnoses
- C3 shows that structured exposure can be part of clinical PFD/ARFID treatment; this does not make generic home repeated offering an adequate substitute for clinical assessment/treatment
- ordinary repeated-exposure advice is therefore kept separate from clinical treatment

## Extraction rules

- effect estimateと95% CIを確認できる場合は併記
- USDA/NESRのGradeはexternal formal ratingとしてoutcome/populationと一緒に引用し、Parenting Evidence独自GRADEへ変換しない
- Appletonはcorrected online articleの数値を使い、erratumを明記する
- willingness to try / liking / intakeを分ける
- target-food effectをoverall picky eatingへ一般化しない
- study-specific exposure countをuniversal thresholdへ変換しない
- contradictory / null evidenceを残す
