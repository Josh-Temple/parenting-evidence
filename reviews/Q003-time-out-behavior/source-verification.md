## Rechecked claims

1. Furlong Cochrane parenting-program evidence
   - original publication is 2012; searches completed January 2011
   - current Cochrane evidence page displays 2022 metadata for the same DOI; this is not treated as a 2022 literature-search update
   - parent-rated conduct problems SMD=-0.53 (95% CI -0.72 to -0.34)
   - independently assessed conduct problems SMD=-0.44 (-0.77 to -0.11)
   - verified
   - remains program-level evidence, not time-out-specific

2. Kaminski et al. 2008
   - time-out was among components associated with larger program effects after design adjustment
   - component association only; not randomized isolation

3. Leijten et al. 2019
   - 154 trials / 398 effect sizes
   - positive reinforcement, praise and natural/logical consequences were the three techniques associated with stronger overall program effects
   - time-out was not one of those three
   - verified

4. Leijten et al. 2018 direct-behavior meta-analysis
   - 19 studies / 75 effect sizes of experimentally manipulated discrete parenting behaviors
   - time-out increased observed and parent-reported compliance
   - reported time-out effects d=0.84-1.72, with confidence intervals across estimates spanning roughly 0.30-2.54
   - added to Q003 because it is a more direct synthesis than relying mainly on a 1976 experiment
   - limitation: relatively few, often old/small studies; short-term compliance outcome

5. Knight et al. 2020
   - parent-reported time-out use was not associated with worse measured long-term emotional/behavioral or parent-child relationship outcomes
   - observational; does not prove zero harm

6. Roach et al. 2025
   - N=474 Australian caregivers
   - appropriate implementation associated with better mental-health / attachment measures; inappropriate implementation with worse outcomes
   - cross-sectional; causal direction unresolved

7. CDC 2026
   - current guidance reserves time-out for serious dangerous/harmful/rule-breaking behavior and continued noncompliance
   - recommends brief, safe, distraction-free use and return to positive reinforcement
   - verified

## Changes made

- Strengthened C003 with Leijten et al. 2018 multilevel meta-analysis.
- Narrowed the direct claim to short-term compliance rather than broad long-term problem-behavior treatment.
- Retained the program/component attribution warning.

## Interpretation check

PASS WITH CHANGES:
