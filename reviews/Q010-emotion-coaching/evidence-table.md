## Quantitative anchor

England-Mason 2023:
- 26 reports
- 15 individual randomized trials
- 9 intervention strategies
- search through 2022-10-05
- age 18 months–6 years 11 months
- emotion socialization parenting practices: g=0.50
- child emotional competence: g=0.44
- positive parenting behavior: g=0.74
- negative parenting behavior: g=0.25
- parent psychological well-being: g=0.28
- child behavioral adjustment: g=0.34
- results remained significant after publication-bias / sensitivity analyses
- authors declared no competing interests

Interpretation:
Intervention-package efficacy, not single-component efficacy.

## Comparator boundary

Many direct TIK studies use waitlist controls.

Waitlist evidence supports benefit relative to waiting/no comparable program.

It does not establish consistent superiority over another credible active parenting program.

C009 remains NOT ESTABLISHED unless direct active-comparator evidence supports superiority.

## Reporter boundary

For child behavior outcomes, preserve:
- parent report
- teacher report
- observation

as separate evidence streams.

Do not treat parent-rated and teacher-rated change as interchangeable.

## Developer / network boundary

TIK-family evidence contains substantial developer/research-team involvement. In the broader 2023 review, 25/33 studies examined TIK variants and Sophie Havighurst was an author on 23/25 of those TIK studies. Among 23 randomized studies, 14 used waitlist controls and 9 used other controls.

This does not invalidate the evidence.

It does mean:
- paper count ≠ independent research tradition count
- repeated TIK positive trials should not be described as fully independent replications

## China 2025 mixed pattern

Immediate reductions in punitive/minimizing reactions and dysfunctional interaction occurred in both intervention and waitlist groups for some outcomes.

Selected delayed program effects were reported.

Do not summarize as uniformly positive.
