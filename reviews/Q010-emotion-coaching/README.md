# Q010 — Emotion coaching / emotion socialization parenting interventions

Parent-facing question:

**子どもの感情を受け止めて言葉にする「emotion coaching」は、幼児の感情調整や行動に役立つか。**

Research state: DRAFT  
Formalized: 2026-09-26

## Scope

Primary population:
- parents/caregivers of children age 18 months–6 years 11 months

Primary intervention:
- multicomponent emotion socialization / emotion-coaching parenting interventions

Core components may include:
- noticing child emotion
- empathy / validation
- emotion labeling
- discussing emotions
- parent emotion regulation
- reducing dismissive/punitive reactions
- problem solving after arousal decreases

Keep separate:
- a single phrase or labeling technique
- ordinary behavioral parenting programs
- attachment programs
- child-only SEL
- parent mental-health treatment
- severe clinical treatment populations
