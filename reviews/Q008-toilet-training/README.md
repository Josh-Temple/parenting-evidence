# Q008 — Toilet training timing, method, and readiness

Parent-facing question:

**トイレトレーニングは、いつ・どの方法で始めるのがよいか。**

Research state: PUBLISHED  
Formalized: 2026-09-26  
Publication Gate: PASSED 2026-09-29

## Scope

Primary population:
- typically developing children, mainly 18 months–4 years
- earlier initiation studies retained for timing questions
- later childhood bladder/bowel outcomes retained only when they inform training timing

Primary questions:
1. Is one training method universally superior?
2. Does starting earlier lead to earlier completion, shorter duration, or better later outcomes?
3. Is there a universal optimal starting age?
4. How should constipation, stool refusal and urinary symptoms change the interpretation?

Separate from primary inference:
- nighttime dryness
- special-needs toilet training
- established enuresis/fecal-incontinence treatment
- clinical constipation treatment efficacy
- elimination communication as a distinct practice unless directly relevant
