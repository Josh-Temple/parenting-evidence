Reader Layer:

### 2. Van Aggelpoel et al. 2021 — specific daycare cluster RCT

Sources:
- PMID 33230718
- DOI 10.1007/s00431-020-03879-y
- NCT04221776

Verified:
- healthy children age 18–30 months
- readiness selection: at least 2 of 3 predefined signs
- 55 analyzed children across 22 daycare centres
- 27 intervention / 28 control
- 2-hour group sessions on 2 consecutive days
- package included underpants, frequent drinking, scheduled potty sitting, response to elimination signals and positive reinforcement
- median daytime-training duration 2 vs 5 weeks
- log-rank p=0.007
- hazard of becoming daytime trained higher during follow-up; p=0.018
- 6-week completion odds higher but not conventionally significant
- 16 daycare centres withdrew after randomization before final collaboration
- delivery unblinded; analysis blinded

Interpretation:
PASS WITH BOUNDARY — programme-specific evidence only. No class-wide superiority claim.

### 3. Blum et al. 2003 — timing trade-off

Source:
- PMID 12671117

Verified:
- 406 enrolled at 17–19 months; 378 followed
- earlier initiation associated with earlier completion
- earlier intensive initiation also associated with longer duration
- intensive start before 27 months did not establish earlier completion in that subgroup

Interpretation:
PASS — start age / completion age / duration remain separate. No 27-month threshold.

### 4. 2020 LUTD systematic review/meta-analysis

Sources:
- PMID 32241587
- DOI 10.1016/j.jpurol.2020.02.016

Verified:
- 10 studies
- N=24,121
- outcome ages 5–17
- pooled OR 0.71 (95% CI 0.63–0.81)
- before-24m subgroup OR 0.77 (95% CI 0.63–0.94)
- no RCTs in the pooled analysis
- heterogeneous definitions of training timing

Interpretation:
PASS — association only; no causal “after 24 months is harmful” wording.

### 5. Constipation / refusal directionality

Blum et al. 2004:
- PMID 15173531
- prospective cohort, 380
- stool refusal 24.4%
- among children with both hard stools and refusal, constipation preceded refusal in 93.4%
- painful defecation preceded refusal in 74.4% of those with both

Naenen et al. 2025:
- PMID 40815791
- DOI 10.1097/DBP.0000000000001370
- Methods describe cross-sectional descriptive design
- N=1,218
- FC 16.8%
- diaper request OR 2.8 (1.8–4.2)
- stool refusal OR 3.2 (2.3–4.3)
- hiding OR 3.7 (2.6–5.4)

Interpretation:
PASS — contemporary association and older temporal evidence are kept distinct.

### 6. Huijgen et al. 2026 — timing variation

Sources:
- PMID 42110126
- DOI 10.1002/jpr3.70163

Verified:

- 60.1% started by 24 months
- 60.0% completed by 36 months
- timing varied with child/social-cultural characteristics

Interpretation:
PASS — descriptive context, not a deadline.
