# Q008 Search Strategy

Last searched: 2026-09-25

## Review type

Targeted evidence review. This is **not** a de novo systematic review.

Primary anchor:
- Mrad et al. 2021 systematic review

Update horizon:
- through 2026-09-25

## Question decomposition

Population:
- typically developing children, mainly 18 months–4 years

Exposure / intervention:
- age of initiation
- child-oriented / readiness-oriented methods
- structured behavioral methods
- specific daycare/group programmes
- parent education

Outcomes:
- daytime urinary continence
- bowel continence
- age at completion
- elapsed training duration
- accidents / refusal / withholding
- functional constipation
- later lower urinary tract dysfunction
- distress / unintended consequences

Nighttime dryness is not treated as equivalent to daytime completion.

## Search streams

1. toilet training method comparison / randomized trial
2. toilet training age of initiation / timing
3. toilet training + lower urinary tract dysfunction
4. toilet training + constipation / refusal / withholding
5. current AAP / HealthyChildren practical guidance
6. Japan official childcare guidance
7. current Japanese clinical constipation-reference metadata

## Japan guidance search

Retained:
- MHLW 保育所保育指針 for individualized, gradual toilet-habit support
- current 2025 Japanese pediatric functional-constipation guideline metadata as the present clinical reference

Important boundary:
The full current Japanese guideline recommendation text was not directly verified through the public path used in this review. No specific clinical recommendation is invented or paraphrased beyond directly verified material.

## Inclusion principles

Include:
- normally developing children
- direct method/timing comparisons
- longitudinal timing evidence
- clinically relevant constipation/refusal associations that affect interpretation
- current practical guidance as a separate guidance layer

Exclude from primary inference:
- neurodevelopmental-disability-specific toilet training
- established enuresis/fecal-incontinence treatment
- nighttime dryness as the same outcome as daytime training
- clinical treatment evidence used as if it were ordinary developmental training evidence

## Interpretation rules

- earlier completion ≠ shorter training duration
- association with LUTD ≠ prevention effect
- readiness guidance ≠ proof of method superiority
- one daycare package ≠ structured-method class effect
- refusal ↔ constipation cross-sectional association does not establish direction
- descriptive completion ages are not deadlines

## Search limitations

- not a de novo systematic search
- no dual screening
- no database-native export/count
- no project-wide formal risk-of-bias instrument
- Japan guideline full recommendation text not directly available through this review path
- culturally variable toilet-training practices limit transportability
