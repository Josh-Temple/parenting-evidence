## U4 cluster-RCT extraction details

Van Aggelpoel 2021:
- age: 18–30 months
- readiness-selected: at least 2 of 3 predefined signs
- final collaboration: 22 daycare centres
- analyzed children: 55
- intervention/control: 27 / 28
- intervention: 2h × 2 consecutive days, small groups
- components: underpants, frequent drinks, scheduled potty sitting, response to elimination signals, positive reinforcement
- median training duration: 2 vs 5 weeks
- log-rank p=0.007
- daytime-training hazard during 6-week follow-up: approximately doubled; p=0.018
- 6-week completion odds: higher in intervention, not conventionally significant
- attrition boundary: 16 daycare centres withdrew after randomization before the final 22-centre collaboration
- blinding: delivery unblinded; analysis blinded

Interpretation:
Specific programme-level positive evidence only. Individual component attribution and class-wide method superiority are not supported.

## Interpretation by claim

### C001 — one universally best method
Status: NOT ESTABLISHED

A1 does not support naming a universal winner. Broad labels contain heterogeneous programmes.

### C001a — specific daycare group programme
Status: SUPPORTED IN ONE SETTING / NOT GENERALIZED

U4 supports shorter daytime training duration in a readiness-selected daycare package. Transportability and component attribution remain limited.

### C002 — earlier initiation / earlier completion / duration
Status: ASSOCIATION / TRADE-OFF

A2 requires three outcomes to remain separate:
- age at initiation
- age at completion
- elapsed duration

### C003 — universal best starting age
Status: NOT ESTABLISHED

No reviewed evidence creates a universal 18m / 24m / 27m cutoff.

### C004 — later initiation causes LUTD
Status: NOT ESTABLISHED CAUSALLY

A3 is association evidence. No RCT was included.

### C005 — constipation / refusal
Status: CLINICALLY IMPORTANT ASSOCIATION WITH TEMPORAL CAUTION

U1 shows contemporary cross-sectional associations; B1 shows constipation/pain can precede refusal.

### C006 — normal timing
Status: WIDE VARIATION / DESCRIPTIVE

U2 provides contemporary population context, not a deadline.

### C007 — readiness
Status: GUIDANCE-SUPPORTED / NOT COMPARATIVE-EFFICACY PROOF

G1/J1 are guidance layers.

### C008 — clinical boundary
Status: SEPARATE

Current Japanese constipation guideline is a clinical reference; unverified recommendation text is not inferred.

## Extraction rules

- daytime and nighttime continence remain separate
- start age, completion age and elapsed duration remain separate
- observational association is not converted to causal benefit/harm
- package interventions are not reduced to single components
- practical guidance is not treated as comparative efficacy evidence
- age distributions are not converted to pass/fail deadlines
