## Interpretation by claim

### C001 — selected cognitive / psychosocial outcomes

Status: ASSOCIATION

- What supports the claim: A1 pooled small associations; J1 Japan-specific developmental association
- What limits the claim: mostly observational evidence; heterogeneity; outcome-specific results
- Comparator: lower/no caregiver use as defined in original studies
- Outcome: cognition / psychosocial measures
- Causal boundary: do not convert pooled association to intervention effect
- Japan applicability: J1 directly relevant, but self-reported maternal exposure and short follow-up

### C002 — long-term causal harm

Status: NOT ESTABLISHED

- What supports concern: A1 associations; J1; acute mechanism studies
- What limits the claim: no evidence isolating long-term causal effect of caregiver technology use itself
- Causal boundary: M1/A2 acute interruption evidence cannot be promoted to long-term developmental harm

### C003 — acute interaction disruption

Status: SUPPORTED — SHORT TERM

- What supports the claim: M1 + A2
- What limits the claim: short-term paradigms; mostly mother-infant evidence
- Outcome: responsiveness / sensitivity / infant affect
- Causal boundary: supports immediate interaction effect, not later developmental diagnosis

### C004 — later child media use

Status: ASSOCIATION WITH LONGITUDINAL EVIDENCE

- What supports the claim: A1 child screen-time association; U5 7-year longitudinal association
- What limits the claim: common reporter / family media ecology / residual confounding
- Japan applicability: direct Japanese longitudinal replication not established

### C005 — universal safe daily threshold

Status: NOT ESTABLISHED

- J1 cutoffs are analytic categories
- no randomized threshold comparison
- no national Japanese caregiver-use cutoff identified in targeted official-source search

### C006 — bidirectionality / shared stress

Status: PLAUSIBLE BIDIRECTIONAL / CONFOUNDED

- U6 and U7 support shared-stress pathways
- temporal direction is not established by cross-sectional evidence
- AAP guidance also recognizes caregiver stress/device-use association

### C007 — simple dose model

Status: NOT SUPPORTED AS UNIVERSAL

- U1 is an important counterexample to a universal “more minutes = worse observed interaction” model
- U9 adds a large 27-country null association for caregiver-use frequency vs motor/executive-function outcomes
- U1 does not test simultaneous active interruption, and U9 is frequency-based / cross-sectional, so neither negates M1/A2

### C008 — consistent language harm

Status: MIXED / NOT ESTABLISHED

- A1 pooled language estimate non-significant
- J1 Japan-specific language-social association
- U2 did not show a consistent longitudinal technoference prediction of later child talk / conversational turns

## Extraction rules

- effect estimateと95% CIを確認できる場合は併記
- observational associationをcausal effectへ変換しない
- short-term interactionとlong-term developmentを分ける
- caregiver useとchild screen exposureを分ける
- study category cutoffをsafety thresholdへ変換しない
- contradictory / null evidenceを残す
